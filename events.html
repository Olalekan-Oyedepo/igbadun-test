Programme Details

Cultural Festival

Our annual cultural festival is a celebration of Yorùbá heritage, bringing together community members and the wider Scottish public for a vibrant showcase of culture, art, and tradition.

Festival Highlights

  • Traditional dance and music performances
  • Art exhibitions featuring local and international artists
  • Yorùbá cuisine tastings and cooking demonstrations
  • Interactive cultural workshops
  • Community recognition awards

Educational Outreach

Our educational outreach programmes bring Yorùbá culture into Scottish schools and institutions, fostering cross-cultural understanding and appreciation.

Programme Components

  • Interactive cultural presentations in schools
  • Academic collaborations with universities
  • Professional development for educators
  • Public lectures and seminars
  • Cultural resource development

Heritage Documentation

We are committed to preserving and documenting Yorùbá cultural heritage in Scotland for future generations.

Documentation Projects

  • Recording oral histories from community elders
  • Creating digital archives of cultural practices
  • Documenting community events and celebrations
  • Publishing community stories and experiences
  • Building an accessible cultural database

Cultural Exchange Programme

Our exchange programmes create meaningful connections between Scottish and Yorùbá communities.

Exchange Activities

  • Organised visits to Yorùbá-speaking regions
  • Virtual connections with cultural organisations
  • Collaborative projects with other cultural groups
  • Youth exchange opportunities
  • Cross-cultural artistic collaborations

Professional Development

We offer opportunities for community members to develop professional skills while preserving cultural practices.

Development Opportunities

  • Workshop series on cultural entrepreneurship
  • Traditional crafts skills development
  • Leadership training programmes
  • Cultural consultation services training
  • Professional networking events

Get Involved

Join us for our upcoming events and be part of our vibrant community. Whether you're interested in attending our festival, participating in our documentation projects, or contributing to our educational programmes, there's a place for you.
